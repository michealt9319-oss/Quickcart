# Refund Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

## Cancelling before delivery

You can cancel your own order, at no charge, any time before it's handed to a rider (i.e.
while its status is "pending," "confirmed," or "packing"). Once it's with a rider, contact us
directly — cancellation may no longer be possible depending on how far delivery has progressed.

A cancelled order is fully refunded to your original payment method within [X business days],
processed via [Paystack/Flutterwave]'s refund system.

## Problems with your delivered order

If your order arrives incomplete, incorrect, or damaged, contact us within [X hours/days] of
delivery at [contact email/WhatsApp number] with your order number and a description (photos
help). We will, at our discretion:
- Refund the affected item(s), or
- Refund the full order, or
- Arrange redelivery of the missing/correct item, where feasible.

## What isn't covered

- Change-of-mind cancellations after your order has been handed to a rider.
- Delays caused by factors outside our reasonable control (traffic, weather, an incorrect
  address provided at checkout).
